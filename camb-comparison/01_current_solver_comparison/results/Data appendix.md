# Data appendix for the solver and emulator comparison

The tables give the numerical results behind the plots. C2 denotes CAMB 2.0.4
and Emu denotes the existing CBL emulator. Each pair is written with the
numerator first. All differences are in percent and k is in Mpc⁻¹. The
maximum is the largest absolute difference among the sampled points. RMS
is the root mean square, mean is signed, and P95 is the 95th percentile of
the absolute differences. Each retained point has equal weight.

The three-way calculation was completed on 7 November 2026. The setup and
interpretation are described in the [detailed report](Report.md).

All differences below are percentages on 0.02 < k < 0.3 Mpc^-1. The k column
is the location of the maximum absolute difference, in Mpc^-1. Mean is signed;
P95 is the 95th percentile of the absolute difference. Maximum and RMS do
not carry a sign. Unrounded values and the sign at the maximum are saved
in the local numerical records.
